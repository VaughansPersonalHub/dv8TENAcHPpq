Supply chain due diligence for acquisitions in Southeast Asia

We validate the operational assumptions behind the deal: network cost, warehouse viability, 3PL contracts and integration risk.

Talk to us about a deal

In most acquisitions, the supply chain is the biggest operational risk and the least well-understood. The deal model assumes cost synergies and service continuity that may not survive contact with reality.

We validate those assumptions. We assess the target's supply chain against operational reality: network cost, warehouse condition, 3PL contract terms, inventory health and integration complexity.

The output is a plain read on what the supply chain will cost to run post-close, where the integration risks sit, and whether the synergy numbers in the deal model are achievable.

What we cover in supply chain due diligence

Our due diligence covers six areas: network design and cost, warehouse and facility assessment, 3PL and contract review, inventory health and obsolescence, systems and data capability, and integration planning.

For each area, we give a red/amber/green rating, quantify the financial exposure, and identify the mitigation actions that would be needed post-close.

Most of our due diligence work is for private equity and corporate acquirers in FMCG, retail and industrial sectors.

Post-merger integration support

The deal closes. The hard work starts. We support the integration planning and execution for the supply chain: network consolidation, warehouse migration, 3PL transition and team integration.

We have managed integrations that involved closing four warehouses and consolidating into two, migrating 200 SKUs between 3PLs, and integrating planning teams across three countries.

The key is planning for operational continuity. Customers do not care about your integration timeline. They care whether their order arrives on time.

What we deliver

  • Operational due diligence report — Six-area assessment with risk ratings and financial exposure.
  • Synergy validation — Independent assessment of cost synergy achievability.
  • Integration roadmap — 100-day plan with risk mitigations and continuity measures.
  • Warehouse and facility assessment — Physical condition, capacity and compliance review.
  • 3PL contract review — Terms, pricing, performance and exit clause analysis.
  • Post-close support — Integration programme management and operational stabilisation.

Frequently asked questions

  • How long does supply chain due diligence take?

    A full operational due diligence takes 3 to 4 weeks from kick-off to report. A rapid diagnostic for a smaller deal can be done in 10 days. We work to deal timelines and can run multiple workstreams in parallel.

  • Do you work with the buy-side or the sell-side?

    Mostly buy-side, for private equity and corporate acquirers. We also do vendor due diligence for sellers who want to present a clean operational picture to the market. We do not work both sides of the same deal.

  • What does a typical supply chain due diligence cost?

    It depends on the number of sites, countries and complexity. A single-country deal with 2-3 warehouses typically runs from MYR 80,000 to 150,000. A multi-country platform deal can be significantly more. We scope and price before we start.

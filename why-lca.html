Why LCA

Independent, operator-led supply chain consulting for Southeast Asia since 1999. We put part of our fee at risk against results.

Five reasons to work with LCA

  • Independent — We do not sell buildings, 3PL contracts, software or automation. Our fee comes only from the client, so our advice is unconflicted.
  • Operators, not theorists — Our team has run warehouses, managed transport fleets and led supply chain functions. We advise from experience, not textbooks.
  • Results-linked fees — Where we are responsible for execution, our fees can be linked to the results. Our incentive aligns with your outcome.
  • Backed by Savills — Savills is our strategic shareholder, giving us access to property market intelligence, funding options and a global network.
  • Client-side, always — We sit on your side of the table. On a build, we check every vendor change order against the contract before you pay.

How we stay independent

LCA Savills is a separate legal entity from Savills plc. Savills is the strategic shareholder which gives us access to property market intelligence, funding capability for built-to-suit projects, and a global client network.

What Savills does not do is tell us what to recommend. Our consulting advice is governed by a professional independence policy that prohibits referral fees, commission arrangements and any financial interest in the outcomes we recommend.

This matters because in supply chain consulting, the conflicts are subtle. A consultant who also sells software will recommend software. A consultant who takes referral fees from 3PLs will recommend those 3PLs. We do neither.

The team

Our team combines deep operational experience with strong analytical capability. Our leaders have run a warehouse, managed a transport fleet or led a supply chain function before joining LCA Savills.

The Savills relationship

Savills is a leading global real estate services firm with 600 offices worldwide and 28,000 professionals in Asia alone. Their investment in LCA reflects a shared belief that supply chain and property decisions should be made together, not in isolation.

For clients, this can mean access to property market intelligence, built-to-suit funding options, and a single point of accountability for projects that span both supply chain design and property delivery.

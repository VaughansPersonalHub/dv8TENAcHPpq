Make your warehouse run better

Lean diagnostics, productivity improvement and continuous improvement systems from people who have run warehouses at scale.

Book a diagnostic

Most warehouses have 15-25% productivity improvement available without new technology. The gap is between how the operation is designed to run and how it actually runs day to day.

We close that gap. We diagnose the operation against lean principles, benchmark against comparable sites, and build an improvement plan that your team can execute and sustain.

The focus is on the basics: layout, slotting, pick paths, labour standards, shift structure and supervisor capability. These are not exciting, but it's where the money is.

What we look for in a warehouse diagnostic

A typical diagnostic covers eight areas: receiving, putaway, storage, picking, packing, shipping, returns and inventory accuracy. For each, we measure current performance and identify the top three opportunities.

The output is a prioritised roadmap with costed initiatives, expected return and a 90-day implementation plan. Most clients see measurable improvement within the first month.

We run the diagnostic with your team, not instead of them. The aim is to build internal capability, not dependency.

Supervisor and team leader development

The best-designed process fails if the supervisor cannot manage it. We run a structured supervisor development programme that covers daily management, problem-solving and continuous improvement tools.

The programme is practical: real problems from your operation, solved during the training. Supervisors leave with a daily management system they can run without us.

This is usually the highest-ROI part of an operational excellence engagement. A good supervisor multiplies the effect of every other improvement.

Frequently asked questions

  • How long does a warehouse diagnostic take?

    A full diagnostic takes 4 to 6 weeks on site. We observe each process, measure cycle times, analyse your WMS data and interview supervisors and operators. The output is a report and a 90-day improvement roadmap, presented to your leadership team.

  • Do you implement the improvements or just recommend?

    We do both. We typically run the first wave of improvements with your team, then coach supervisors to sustain and extend the gains. Most clients want us involved for 3 to 9 months to embed the new way of working.

  • What about automation? Should we automate?

    Maybe — we also have automation warehouse design and delivery services, so we can help you understand the business case. Additionally, many warehouses also have significant improvement available from better process, layout and management. We will tell you honestly whether automation is the next logical step.

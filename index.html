Frequently asked questions

  • What does a supply chain and logistics consultant in Asia actually do?

    It depends on the problem. We help you decide where stock and capacity should sit, balance inventory against service, take cost out of transport and last mile, select and contract 3PLs, and improve facilities you already run. We also design, build and commission distribution centres end to end. We do not operate your warehouse long-term.

  • Is LCA only a warehouse consultancy?

    No. Warehousing end to end is our signature work, but it is one capability within a broader consultancy. We also cover network and supply chain strategy, planning and inventory, procurement and 3PL, transport and last mile, cost-to-serve, operational excellence and M&A support across Asia.

  • How is LCA independent across all of this?

    We do not sell buildings, 3PL contracts, software or automation, so we have nothing to push. On a network study, a tender, an inventory fix or a warehouse build alike, the recommendation is the one that is right for you. Savills is a strategic shareholder that adds property and funding reach, and the independence is paramount.

  • Which countries in Asia does LCA cover?

    We live and work in Asia. Core markets include: China, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, and Vietnam. Local rules vary, so we factor land tenure, permitting and lease terms into the decision before anything is signed.

  • Will LCA link its fees to results?

    Yes. We will put part of our fee against delivering a target we have set, such as a transport-cost or service-level gain.
